Privacy

Privacy Policy

Effective November 12, 2026

OC Scanner is an independent news and monitoring outlet run from Orange County, California. This page explains what information we get when you use ocscanner.media, send us a tip, or sign in to our staff tools, and what we do with it. The short version: we collect very little, we don't sell it, and we don't use advertising trackers.

Reading the site

  • No accounts, no ad trackers, no cookies from us. You can read everything on ocscanner.media without signing up.
  • Visitor counts. We use Cloudflare Web Analytics to see things like page views, which pages are popular, and what country visits come from. It does not use cookies and is not used to identify you.
  • Hosting and security logs. The site runs on Cloudflare. Like any web host, Cloudflare processes your IP address and basic browser details to deliver pages, block abuse and keep the site secure. Our own news feed also uses your IP address briefly to limit how fast one visitor can make requests; we don't store it.
  • Settings saved in your browser. The site remembers your light or dark theme choice and keeps short-lived copies of feeds (news, launches, the daily digest) in your browser's storage so pages load faster. This stays on your device and is never sent to us. Clearing your browser data removes it.
  • Fonts. Our fonts load from Google Fonts, so your browser connects to Google when a page opens.

Embedded and linked services

Some pages show content from other services, and some buttons take you to them. When you use them, their privacy policies apply, not ours:

  • YouTube — our live stream player on the Live page.
  • MaxQ — launch information and players on the Space and Live pages.
  • Public data sources — weather, aircraft, launch and news feeds shown on the site. Some are loaded through our own server, so the source sees our server, not you; others, such as National Weather Service data, load directly in your browser.
  • X, Instagram and other links — when you follow a link to a social network or another news site, you leave ocscanner.media.
  • Donations and the store — payments through Venmo, Ko-fi or our Fourthwall shop are handled entirely by those companies. We never see or store your card or bank details. We may see the name or message you choose to share with a donation or order.

Sending a tip

Tips come in by email to OCScannerNews@gmail.com. We receive whatever you send: your email address, your name if you include it, your message, and any photos or video.

  • We use tips to check and report the news. Sending a tip allows us to use the information in our reporting.
  • We don't name tipsters unless you ask us to credit you. If you send footage and want credit, tell us how you'd like to be named.
  • Photos and videos can carry hidden details, such as the location where they were taken. If that matters to you, turn off location for your camera or send a screenshot instead.
  • Tips are kept in our email inbox, which is protected with two-step sign-in. Ask us and we'll delete your messages.
  • Want to stay anonymous? Use an email address that doesn't show your name, and leave your name out. Never put yourself in danger for a tip. In an emergency, call 911.

Staff and contributors

Our newsroom tools (the Staff Login link) are for invited staff only. If you're one of them:

  • You sign in with your Google account through Cloudflare Access. Google tells us your name and email address; we don't receive your password. Cloudflare keeps a record of sign-ins (time, email and IP address) for security.
  • We store your name, email address, role, the stories and photos you add, and a log of newsroom actions (such as drafting, editing and publishing) so we can see who changed what.
  • When you publish, your name appears as the story's byline, along with any photo caption and credit you add.
  • Photo files are kept as uploaded. If location data is turned on in your camera, turn it off before uploading, or check the photo first.
  • If you leave, we turn off your access. Stories you wrote stay published with your byline unless we agree otherwise.

AI tools

We use AI services, including Anthropic's Claude, for tasks such as the daily news digest on the home page. That digest is built from public headlines. We don't send visitors' information to AI services, and every published story is reviewed by a person.

What we don't do

  • We don't sell or rent personal information, and we don't share it for advertising.
  • We don't build profiles of visitors.
  • We share information only with the service providers named on this page so they can run the site, when the law requires it, or to protect someone's safety.

Your choices and rights

You can ask us what personal information we have about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it, by emailing OCScannerNews@gmail.com with "Privacy request" in the subject. California residents have these rights under state law, and we extend them to everyone. We won't treat you differently for asking. We may need to confirm it's really you before acting on a request.

Children

ocscanner.media is a general news site and isn't aimed at children under 13. We don't knowingly collect personal information from them. If you think a child has sent us personal information, contact us and we'll delete it.

Keeping information safe

Our staff tools sit behind Cloudflare Access, staff sign in with Google, our accounts use two-step sign-in, and the site is served only over encrypted connections. No system is perfectly secure, but we keep what we collect small and limit who can see it.

Changes to this policy

If we change how we handle information, we'll update this page and the date at the top.

Contact

Questions about privacy: OCScannerNews@gmail.com.

Important

OC Scanner is an independent news and monitoring account. We are not affiliated with any law enforcement, fire, or government agency. Information is shared for situational awareness and may be preliminary or unconfirmed. In an emergency, always call 911.
