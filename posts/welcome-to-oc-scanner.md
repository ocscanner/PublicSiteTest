*First, thank you. To everyone who follows, shares, sends tips, picks up gear or chips in to keep OC Scanner running: none of this exists without you. This site is built for you.*

Welcome to ocscanner.media, the home base for OC Scanner. If you follow us on X or Instagram, you know what we do: when something happens in Orange County, Southern California, or anywhere in the world that matters here, we get it out fast and we get it right. This site is where you'll find the live picture, longer reports, and the tools that don't fit in a single post.

OC Scanner is run independently by a U.S. Army veteran. We cover public safety, national security, intel and geopolitical events, pairing live scanner traffic with OSINT: public, checkable information from many sources. More than 95,000 of you follow along across X and Instagram, and we don't take that for granted.

## The homepage

![The OC Scanner homepage, with The Briefing beside The Wire](posts/img/welcome/homepage.jpg)

**The Briefing** is a short, plain-English summary of what's moving right now, pulled from public news feeds and refreshed about every eight hours. It's labeled as an AI-assisted summary: a starting point, not verification.

**The Wire** is a live feed of headlines from local, national and world outlets, refreshed about every 15 minutes and linked to the original source. Filter it by Public Safety, Traffic, Fire, Politics, World, Geopolitics or POTUS. Its tags are applied automatically and aren't editorial confirmation; when we've verified something, you'll hear it from OC Scanner.

## News

**News** is our original reporting. Every report carries a byline, publish and update dates, a dateline, and a way to request a correction. This is the first one.

## Elections

The **Elections** page has live results for the Nov. 3 midterms, nationally and here in Orange County. We'll walk through it in our next report.

## Live and Space

When we go live, the stream runs on the **Live** page and on X at the same time.

![The Space page: upcoming launches and ISS passes over Orange County](posts/img/welcome/space.jpg)

**Space** tracks upcoming launches from California, Texas and Florida, with countdowns and launch status, and the next times the International Space Station passes overhead. It defaults to Orange County, or tap **Use my location**. Launch dates slip often, so confirm with the provider before making plans.

## Community and partners

![The Community page and Insider Wire, OC Scanner's news partner](posts/img/welcome/community.jpg)

The **Community** page highlights the platforms we work with:

## Send a tip

Seeing something develop, or have footage? Email **[OCScannerNews@gmail.com](mailto:OCScannerNews@gmail.com)**. Photos, video, locations and times help us verify faster, and you can stay anonymous. Never put yourself in harm's way for a tip. OC Scanner isn't affiliated with any law enforcement, fire or government agency; in an emergency, call 911.

## How we report

Three rules, laid out on our **About & Standards** page: **verify** before posting and label anything unconfirmed, **show the work** behind what we report, and **correct in the open** when the facts change. Hold us to it.
