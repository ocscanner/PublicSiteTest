About

The desk
OC Scanner
Founder & operator · U.S. Army veteran
  • Independent & unaffiliated
  • OSINT-driven, verify-first
  • Monitoring OC, SoCal & beyond
  • 84K+ following across X & IG

OC Scanner started with a scanner, a notebook, and a simple frustration: by the time most local stories reached the public, they were hours old and missing context. I set out to fix that — monitoring public safety, national security, intel, and geopolitical developments across Orange County, Southern California, and beyond, and getting verified information out while it still matters.

As an Army veteran, I take sourcing seriously. Everything here runs on open-source methods: corroborate first, label what's still developing, and correct in the open. No affiliation, no agenda — just a clear signal in a noisy feed, available to anyone who wants to know what's happening around them.

— OC Scanner
