Election 2026

Midterm results

Every House seat, Senate race and governor’s race on Nov. 3, plus California’s 14 propositions, updated about every 10 minutes as states count.

Vote counts come from state election offices and are unofficial until each state certifies. States without a live results feed are filled in by the OC Scanner desk. Race calls are made by OC Scanner; a seat shows as won only once it has been called.

Important

OC Scanner is an independent news and monitoring account. We are not affiliated with any law enforcement, fire, or government agency. Information is shared for situational awareness and may be preliminary or unconfirmed. In an emergency, always call 911.
