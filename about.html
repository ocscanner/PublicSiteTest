OC Scanner started with a scanner, a notebook, and a simple frustration: by the time most local stories reached the public, they were hours old and missing context. I set out to fix that — monitoring public safety, national security, intel, and geopolitical developments across Orange County, Southern California, and beyond, and getting verified information out while it still matters.

As an Army veteran, I take sourcing seriously. Everything here runs on open-source methods: corroborate first, label what's still developing, and correct in the open. No affiliation, no agenda — just a clear signal in a noisy feed, available to anyone who wants to know what's happening around them.

— OC Scanner

How we report

Verified before it's published

OC Scanner runs on open-source methods and one standard: get it right, show the work, and own the mistakes.

Verify

Confirm, then post

We corroborate through multiple signals before publishing. If something isn't confirmed, we label it clearly.

Source

Show the work

Open-source intelligence and scanner traffic — we point to what we're seeing wherever we can.

Correct

Update in the open

Developing stories change. When the facts shift, we update and correct publicly.

Important

OC Scanner is an independent news and monitoring account. We are not affiliated with any law enforcement, fire, or government agency. Information is shared for situational awareness and may be preliminary or unconfirmed. In an emergency, always call 911.
